# How to Play
## Saves
Roll a d20. If it’s equal to or under the relevant Ability Score, you succeed. 1 always succeeds. 20 always fails.

## Turns & Actions
Players go before enemies, unless surprised.<br>
Roll a DEX Save to act on the first turn.<br>
Players take turns in any order.<br>

## Actions
On your turn, you can move and take one action:<br>
attack, flee, move, etc.

## Saves
For risky actions, roll a Save.<br>
* STR Save: Lifting something heavy
* DEX Save: Dodging

## Attacks
Do not roll to hit, instead roll your weapon’s damage die, unless:<br>
* Impaired (bad angle or grappled): roll d2.
* Enhanced (helpless target or cool stunt): roll d12.

When someone attempts a non-damaging maneuver, like throwing mud or sand in an enemy’s eyes, the target makes a DEX Save.

## Damage
Lose HP first. If HP hits 0, subtract further damage from STR. When you take STR damage, roll a STR Save or suffer Critical Damage.

## Critical Damage
When you take Critical Damage you are unable to act until after a short rest.

## Death
If STR hits 0, you’re dead.<br>
Generate a new character and have them pop in as soon as possible. Don’t worry about making their arrival realistic. Waiting at the table is boring.

## Environmental Damage
In most cases, environmental damage ranges from d4 to d12. Use d20 for truly deadly stuff like big falling boulders.<br>
Fire deals d6 damage per round while a creature or PC is exposed to the flames.

## Resting & Recovery
One ration and a sip of water restores all HP. A week of downtime in safety restores all damage dealt to STR or DEX.

## Deprived
Without water or food you will be Deprived and gain no benefits from resting.
