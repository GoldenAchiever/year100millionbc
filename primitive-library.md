## Third Party Modules:

### <a href="https://mad-about-mushrooms.itch.io/1000-meters-below">1000 Meters Below</a>  
A 30-page megadungeon with Carboniferous vibes, packed with giant insects, mushrooms, and sprawling underground caves.
  
### <a href="https://xiaofang64.itch.io/chum">C.H.U.M.</a>  
A pamphlet adventure that introduces elegant raft mechanics. A gigantic sea creature is terrorizing the neighborhood.

### <a href="https://xiaofang64.itch.io/lair-of-the-hillcarver">Lair of the Hillcarver</a>   
A pamphlet adventure about claiming a new home by driving out its current occupant, a mighty Megatherium known as the Hillcarver. Don’t let its vegetarian diet fool you. Those claws hurt.

### <a href="https://sam-worthington.itch.io/the-cave-of-too-many-legs">The Cave of Too-Many-Legs</a>   
A giant centipede’s mountain cave contains what could be the greatest feast ever, if you can survive getting to the eggs. Along the way you’ll encounter a Man-Eater cult, sapient gibbons, desperate swamp dwellers, and more.

### <a href="https://gwannon.itch.io/murder-she-painted-in-the-walls-of-the-cave">Murder, she painted in the walls of the cave</a>   
Hack for 100 Million B.C. where your cavemen become detectives who must solve a crime.

### <a href="https://petrichor-night.itch.io/those-old-bones">Those Old Bones</a>   
An expansion adding mystical and divine elements, along with building and developing a shared community.

### <a href="https://rise-press.itch.io/uglug-need-color">Uglug Need Color</a>   
Uglug is a strange but talented cave painter, and he needs more colors.Players need to find new pigments and help Uglug create an epic prehistoric masterpiece.

### <a href="https://hellohatti.itch.io/ugg-not-cub">Ugg Not Cub!</a>   
Your dear friend Ugg has been mistaken for a Furclaw’s cub and carried back to its den. Find the den and rescue your friend before Mama Furclaw realizes her mistake...

### <a href="https://wyvernden.itch.io/the-wise-one">Wise One</a>   
A character add-on for playing a prehistoric genius with a touch of the supernatural.

### <a href="https://roque-romero.itch.io/year-100-million-bc-reglas-gratuitas">Year 100 Million B.C. - Reglas Gratuitas</a>   
A free Spanish translation of the core rules.

### <a href="https://el-dado-inquieto.itch.io/year-100-million-bc-taules">Year 100 Million B.C. - Regles Gratuïtes</a>  
A free Catalan translation of the core rules.
